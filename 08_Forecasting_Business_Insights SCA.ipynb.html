# 90-Day Forecast Stability Analysis

forecast_values = forecast_df["Forecasted_Demand"]

forecast_mean = forecast_values.mean()
forecast_std = forecast_values.std()
forecast_min = forecast_values.min()
forecast_max = forecast_values.max()

forecast_range = forecast_max - forecast_min
coefficient_of_variation = (forecast_std / forecast_mean) * 100

print("90-Day Forecast Stability Analysis")
print("=" * 60)

print(f"Average Forecasted Demand      : {forecast_mean:.2f}")
print(f"Forecast Standard Deviation    : {forecast_std:.2f}")
print(f"Minimum Forecasted Demand      : {forecast_min:.2f}")
print(f"Maximum Forecasted Demand      : {forecast_max:.2f}")
print(f"Forecast Range                 : {forecast_range:.2f}")
print(f"Coefficient of Variation (CV)  : {coefficient_of_variation:.2f}%")

if coefficient_of_variation < 5:
    stability_status = "Highly Stable"
elif coefficient_of_variation < 10:
    stability_status = "Moderately Stable"
else:
    stability_status = "Highly Variable"

print(f"\nForecast Stability Status     : {stability_status}")

Supply Chain Demand Forecasting & Anomaly Detection¶

Day –9 :- Demand Forecasting & Business Insights¶

Objective¶

Develop and evaluate demand forecasting models and generate a reliable 90-day demand forecast to support inventory, procurement, and replenishment planning.

1. Historical Demand Preparation¶

  • Loaded the final feature-engineered supply chain dataset.
  • Dataset shape: 83,750 rows × 33 columns
  • Created historical daily demand aggregation.
  • Historical demand period:
    • Start: 2024-01-31
    • End: 2024-12-30
  • Generated 335 daily demand observations.

2. Forecasting Dataset Preparation¶

  • Created a chronological train-test split.
  • Historical demand was divided into:
    • Training: 268 observations
    • Testing: 67 observations
  • Maintained chronological order to prevent data leakage.

3. Baseline Forecasting Models¶

Evaluated:

  • 7-Day Moving Average
  • 30-Day Moving Average

Performance:

Model MAE RMSE MAPE
7-Day Moving Average 147.29 169.11 3.93%
30-Day Moving Average 476.63 505.14 12.47%

The 7-Day Moving Average achieved the lowest RMSE and was selected as the best baseline forecasting model.

4. ARIMA Forecasting¶

Implemented and evaluated ARIMA(1,1,1).

Performance:

  • MAE: 1053.89
  • RMSE: 1264.51
  • MAPE: 25.74%

ARIMA performed worse than the 7-Day Moving Average on the selected test period.

5. Final Forecast Model Selection¶

Final model selected:

7-Day Moving Average

Reason:

  • Lowest MAE
  • Lowest RMSE
  • Lowest MAPE

6. 90-Day Demand Forecast¶

Generated a recursive 90-day future demand forecast.

Forecast period:

  • Start: 2024-12-31
  • End: 2025-03-30

Forecast statistics:

  • Average Demand: 4,943.11 units/day
  • Minimum: 4,906.86 units/day
  • Maximum: 4,953.76 units/day
  • Standard Deviation: 5.14
  • Coefficient of Variation: 0.10%

7. Forecast Stability Analysis¶

The forecast was classified as:

Highly Stable

First 7-Day Average: 4,936.32 units

Last 7-Day Average: 4,943.79 units

Overall change: +7.47 units / +0.15%

Therefore, demand is expected to remain broadly stable throughout the forecast horizon.

8. Inventory & Demand Planning Benchmarks¶

Estimated demand benchmarks:

  • 7-Day Demand: 34,601.76 units
  • 30-Day Demand: 148,293.27 units
  • 90-Day Demand: 444,879.82 units

These values provide demand-planning benchmarks for procurement and replenishment decisions.

9. Historical + Forecast Timeline¶

Combined:

  • 335 historical observations

  • Missing values: 0

  • Continuous historical-to-forecast timeline maintained.

10. Business Insight¶

The selected forecasting model indicates relatively stable future demand around 4.9K–5.0K units per day. This supports predictable procurement and replenishment planning while inventory levels, supplier lead times, and previously detected anomalies should continue to be monitored.

Key Outcome¶

The forecasting stage successfully produced a validated 90-day demand forecast using the best-performing 7-Day Moving Average model, providing a practical foundation for supply-chain planning and the final business intelligence stage.
